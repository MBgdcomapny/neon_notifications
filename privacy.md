5. Online features and how to turn them off
The game works offline. Online features are: sending statistics, sending messages, and downloading news notices. You can turn all of them off with the "Internet connection" switch in Settings: the game then contacts no server and only shows previously saved notices.

9. Your rights
For contact form data, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability (Arts. 15–20 GDPR). To exercise them, write to mbgamingsociety@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority in your country of residence.
10. Changes
If significant changes are made, the updated version will be published on the game's page with the new date.
